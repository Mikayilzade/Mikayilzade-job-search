# Candidate Profile

## Baseline
- Location: Baku, Azerbaijan.
- Target: at least 2,500 AZN net/month equivalent; prefer 3,000–5,000+ AZN or strong upside.
- Work model: Baku office/hybrid, Azerbaijan-eligible remote, international contractor/EOR, or strong relocation opportunity.
- English: approximately B2; Azerbaijani native; Russian advanced.

## Core experience
- ~9 years in Accounts Payable / Finance Operations.
- SAP ECC and SAP S/4HANA / MDG.
- Vendor/master data, bank details, payment terms/methods, invoice processing, payment proposals.
- Reconciliations, intercompany work, month-end support, GR/IR, aged items, audit support.
- Advanced Excel, VBA, Power Query/Pivot; strong automation/process-improvement orientation.
- Experience working across multinational teams and coordinating with GL, Tax, AR, buyers, vendors, managers, and other stakeholders.
- Strong operational ownership, data checking, documentation, issue resolution, and process standardization.

## Transferable strengths
- High-volume transactional operations.
- KPI/reporting and large-data handling.
- Process mapping, controls, exception handling, workflow improvement.
- ERP/process discipline and master-data quality.
- Cross-functional coordination and escalation.
- Automation mindset and practical problem solving.
- Ability to learn adjacent operational domains quickly.

## Search domains
Do not limit to AP titles. Include:
- AP / P2P / Finance Operations / AR / O2C / Reconciliations.
- Vendor / Master Data / ERP / SAP operations and support.
- Finance analyst, reporting, budgeting, controls where transition gap is reasonable.
- Procurement analytics / purchasing operations / P2P analytics.
- Supply chain / inventory / warehouse operations / operations management when skills transfer.
- Business / operations / process analyst; BPM; continuous improvement.
- Project / program / delivery coordination or management when role is process-heavy rather than deeply technical.
- Implementation / onboarding / customer operations / customer success for B2B/process-heavy products.
- Sales operations / account management / selected B2B sales roles where process, data, ERP, stakeholder management dominate over cold selling.
- Other unusual roles that match the skill graph even if the title sounds unrelated.

## Avoid by default
- Roles clearly below compensation target unless exceptional upside is documented.
- Native-level English or heavy public-speaking roles where language is the primary competency.
- Pure software engineering, deep technical infrastructure, legal, tax-specialist, engineering, or regulated-profession roles without a plausible bridge.
- High-pressure 24/7/on-call jobs unless compensation and upside justify the tradeoff.
